# Privacy Policy — Pocket Grimoire

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Your spiritual intimacy is sacred — and so is your data. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and what we NEVER do.

## 1. Summary in one candle

- Your records (spells, diaries, dreams, reflections) stay on YOUR device.
- Cloud sync does not depend on your plan: it works on any account, comes turned on, and can be turned off whenever you want. It is protected by rules that let only YOU reach your data.
- We do not sell your data. Ever.
- Texts and photos sent to the Mystic Counselor are processed to generate the answer and are not used for any other purpose by the application. Two providers do the processing: Groq and Google (Gemini).
- The palm reading photo is processed on the spot and discarded — it is not stored.
- The Menstrual Cycle record is health data and has a rule of its own: it stays on the device, and the general sync does not carry it. It only leaves here with the yes you give when you start recording, a yes separate from the terms you accepted — and the pages each day earns in your Grimoire do not leave even then.
- The photos of the entries you create in the encyclopedia go to a private space in your account (reachable by you alone) when you are logged in with sync on — that way they appear on all your devices; without an account, or with sync off, they stay on the device only. The profile photo stays on the device on the phone, and in that private space on the browser.

## 2. What we collect, and why

### Account data
Display name, e-mail and credentials — for authentication and access recovery. Using the application requires an account (see Terms of Use, section 3). Provider: Supabase.

### Content you create
Spells, diaries (dreams, desires, gratitude, affirmations, reflections), sigils, rituals and the records of the practices you complete, saved readings (tarot, runes, oracle, pendulum, palmistry), the lessons of the Living Grimoire and the advice you keep from the Mystic Counselor — reflections, lessons and reading pages all live in the same collection —, the entries you create in the encyclopedia, the day's check-in and magical weather, your progress in the learning trails, the Cycle Reading, the Menstrual Cycle record (which has a rule of its own, just below), birth charts, magical profile and preferences (theme, language, form of address).

Stored locally on the device and sent to your account's cloud while sync is on — on any plan, free or Premium. Turning sync off stops the sending.

The **photos you attach** also belong here. The photos of the entries you create in the encyclopedia are sent to a private storage of your account (Supabase Storage) when you are logged in with sync on — that is what makes the page appear with its photo on your other devices; a compressed copy also stays on the device, so it opens without a network. In herb entries, that photo is also sent to the vision provider before being stored: to identify the plant, when you mark that you do not know its name, and to write the entry anchored in the specimen you photographed. In the crystal entry, the page is written from the name and the photo does not go to the AI — see section 3. Without an account, or with sync off, they stay on the device only. The profile image stays on the device on the phone and, on the browser (where that local folder does not exist), in that same private storage — see section 4.

### Menstrual Cycle record — sensitive health data (optional)

Bleeding dates, intensity, symptoms, mood and your notes — what you record in the Menstrual Cycle wheel, and nothing beyond that. The application does not calculate averages, cycle day or a forecast of the next date. What it does calculate, on the device itself and only from the days you marked, is what the card "The Moon and you" shows: the Moon phase in which each of your bleedings began, and a tally of the moods you noted most on bleeding days. Those counts do not leave here and do not become any forecast.

This record is health information. The LGPD (Brazil's General Data Protection Law) classifies health data as **sensitive personal data** (art. 5, II) and handles it through separate, specific consent — specific to this purpose, apart from the other authorizations. That is how it works here, and the rule is narrower than the one for the rest of the content you create:

- **There is one yes, and it starts out turned off.** The door of the Menstrual Cycle explains what the record does and what stays where; by accepting, you authorize **recording** and **keeping the record in your account's cloud**, in that same gesture. This is a separate, specific consent, and it is not included in the terms you accepted when signing up. Turning on the application's general sync (Settings → Sync and Backup) does **not** turn this sending on: without this yes, the record does not leave the device, on any plan, with the general sync on or off. Refusing erases nothing and blocks nothing else in the application.
- **What goes up, if and for as long as you keep sending turned on:** the date of each recorded day, the mark you chose (started, flow day, spotting, ended, or just a note), the intensity, the symptoms, the mood, your note, and the row's control data (how many times it was changed, whether it was erased, and the creation and change dates). Destination: your account's cloud at Supabase, under the same per-account isolation rules that hold for everything else — only you reach it.
- **What does NOT leave, not even with sending on:** the page that each recorded day earns in your Grimoire, under "My Records". It is a local mirror, written in prose, and it does not leave this device under any circumstances.
- **Turning it off stops the sending and erases the copy.** The switch lives in Settings → Privacy. From the moment you turn it off, nothing new leaves the device and nothing comes down from the cloud, and the copy that was in your account is erased — the confirmation says both things before you turn it off. If the network fails right then, the request is recorded and completed as soon as there is a connection. The days stay on this device.
- **Erasing a day, and what stays.** Erasing a day in the wheel removes what you wrote in it — the mark, the intensity, the symptoms, the mood and the note — here and, if sending is on, in your account as well. What stays is that day's date, marked as erased: it is what keeps another device of yours, holding an old copy, from bringing the day back. That mark goes away when you erase everything, when you erase the cloud copy, or when you delete the account.
- **Erasing everything.** In Settings → Privacy, "Clear Local Data" erases the recorded days and their pages in the Grimoire on this device; turning the Menstrual Cycle switch off erases what is on the server; and deleting the account (Profile → Manage Your Data) removes both sides.
- **It only reaches artificial intelligence through an explicit request and through a single path**: you authorizing the Menstrual Cycle as a source of a Cycle Reading and marking, day by day, what goes along. Your words (each day's note) still depend on a second switch, apart. Outside of that, nothing from this record is sent to any provider.
- **It comes out whole whenever you want.** The export carries the complete record, and Settings → Privacy has a button that erases only it — the days, their pages in the Grimoire and the Cycle Reading reports that carried them along —, without touching the rest of your data.

### Birth data (optional)
Date, time and place — used exclusively for astrology and numerology calculations.

### Payment data
Subscriptions are processed by the store (Google Play/App Store) via RevenueCat. We have no access to your card number.

### Minimal technical data
Error logs for diagnostics and aggregated usage statistics (if enabled in the privacy settings), without personal content from your records.

## 3. The Mystic Counselor (intelligent features)

To generate answers (spells, interpretations of dreams and spreads, numerology explanations, palm reading, plant identification from the photo, writing the herb entry from the photo), the text you provide — and, when the feature uses a photo, the image — is securely sent to a processing provider and used only to generate that answer.

There are two providers: Groq, which serves the text requests, and Google (Gemini), which serves the requests with a photo. If the chosen provider fails, the same request is remade on the other — which is why text and photo may pass through either of the two.

- The palm photo is resized on the device, sent, processed and discarded — it is not saved anywhere.
- Your personal notes are NEVER sent without you triggering a Counselor feature.
- The Menstrual Cycle record is only sent inside a Cycle Reading that you authorized, with the days you marked — see section 2.
- The language and form of address you chose accompany the request, to personalize the answer.

## 4. Where your data lives

- **On the device**: local database (SQLite) and preferences. Uninstalling the app without a backup erases this data.
- **In the cloud (optional, on any plan)**: Supabase, with Row Level Security — each account sees only its own records. The Menstrual Cycle record only arrives here if you turn on the sending of it, which is a separate yes and comes turned off (see section 2); its pages in the Grimoire stay OUT of here always, on any plan and with any switch on.
- **Attached photos**: private storage at Supabase Storage — the photos of the encyclopedia entries on any platform (with an account and sync on; without that, on the device only) and the profile photo when used on the browser. The files live in a folder identified by your account, and the server rules prevent one person from reaching another's folder. The storage is not public: to display the image, the application generates a temporary address, valid for about an hour (on the phone, the local copy makes that unnecessary). When you remove the photo or the entry in the application, the file is erased from the storage.

## 5. Sharing

We share data only with the operators necessary for the app to work: Supabase (account and sync), RevenueCat/stores (subscriptions), Groq and Google (Mystic Counselor answers). We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

## 6. Your rights (LGPD)

At any time, you may:

- **Access and export** your data (Profile → Manage Your Data → Export);
- **Correct** profile information;
- **Delete** local data and/or the account along with the data in the cloud (Profile → Manage Your Data);
- **Disable** statistics and error reports in the privacy settings;
- **Revoke** cloud sync whenever you want (Settings → Sync and Backup).

Deleting the account removes your data from the cloud; local data is under your direct control.

## 7. Device permissions

- **Camera/Gallery**: for the palm reading (Premium), the profile photo and the photos of the entries you create in the encyclopedia, always by an action of yours.
- **Notifications**: moon and sabbat reminders, if you allow them.
- **Gallery (saving)**: exporting sigils, by an action of yours.
